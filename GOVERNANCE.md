# Governance: The Neutral Lab Model

**ThaiOpenGovLab** is a personal, community-led initiative focused on creating open government standards. This document outlines our informal governance model, ensuring neutrality and openness.

## 🏛️ Foundational Principles

### 1. Independence & Personal Initiative

ThaiOpenGovLab is a personal project intended to serve as a neutral playground for public sector innovation. It is not a legal entity and does not represent any specific government or organization.

### 2. Openness by Default

All standards, documentation, and tools produced by the lab are open-source and free for anyone to use, modify, and distribute.

### 3. Machine-First Transparency

We prioritize structured, machine-readable formats to ensure that transparency is efficient and verifiable.

### 4. Meritocratic Collaboration

As this is a community-driven initiative, participation is open to everyone. Contributions are welcomed based on their alignment with the lab's mission.

## 🤝 Community Structure

- **Owner/Maintainer**: The individual(s) leading the initiative and managing the GitHub organization.
- **Contributors**: Anyone who submits code, research, or feedback.

---

_This governance model is informal and intended to guide the community's growth while preserving neutrality._
