# Contributing to ThaiOpenGovLab

Thank you for your interest in **ThaiOpenGovLab**. We are a personal, community-driven initiative dedicated to building transparent, machine-readable, and neutral standards for the public sector.

As a neutral lab, we welcome contributions from everyone—developers, researchers, policy experts, and government officials from any country.

## 🌟 How Can I Contribute?

### 🛠️ Code & Standards

- Propose new standards or improvements to existing ones (e.g., TOR schemas).
- Fix bugs or improve functionality in our repositories.
- Enhance documentation to make standards easier to adopt.

### 🔬 Research & Analysis

- Share case studies of open government implementations.
- Research best practices for public sector digital transformation.
- Contribute to whitepapers and policy recommendations.

### 🌍 Translation & Localization

- Help translate our standards and guides into different languages to broaden their impact.

## 🚀 Getting Started

1.  **Find an Issue**: Browse the issues in our repositories or start a discussion if you have a new idea.
2.  **Fork the Repo**: Create your own copy of the repository to work on.
3.  **Branching**: Work on a descriptive branch name (e.g., `feature/new-tor-schema` or `fix/broken-link`).
4.  **Commit Messages**: Use clear, concise commit messages.
5.  **Submit a Pull Request**: When you're ready, submit a PR for review.

## 🤝 Community Engagement

- Join our discussions on GitHub.
- Reach out via the channels mentioned in [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md).

## ⚖️ Neutrality & Collaboration

ThaiOpenGovLab is intentionally independent of any single organization or state. We maintain neutrality to ensure that our work serves the global public interest. All contributions must align with these principles of openness and inclusivity.

_By contributing, you agree that your work will be licensed under the repository's open-source license._
