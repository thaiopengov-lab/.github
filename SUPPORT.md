# Support

We are here to help you get the most out of **ThaiOpenGovLab**.

## 💬 Community Support

- **GitHub Discussions**: The primary place for community engagement. Use this for general questions, ideas, and broad discussions.
- **GitHub Issues**: Use issues for reporting specific bugs or suggesting concrete features.

## 🏛️ Project Documentation

Each sub-project within ThaiOpenGovLab (e.g., [AI Friendly TOR](../ai-friendly-tor)) includes its own specific documentation. Always check the project's root directory for detailed guides.

---

## 🚀 How You Can Help Us

As a personal, community-led project, we rely on your contributions! If you find a solution to a problem, please consider contributing to our documentation or starting a discussion thread to help others.
